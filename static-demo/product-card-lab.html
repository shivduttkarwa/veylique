Product card lab

Five directions for the Veylique product card, rendered with the real catalogue photography, the real palette, and Clash Display — so you are judging the actual thing, not a description. Hover any card to see the second-image swap, the wishlist and the add-to-bag behaviour.

How to use this: hover a few cards in each block, then tell me a letter — or mix (“C’s image ratio, D’s info bar”). Nothing here is live. Once you pick, I port it into snippets/veylique-home-product-card.liquid and it applies everywhere at once: home carousels, collection, search, and “You may also like”.
